# Mass Spectrometry Course Materials

## Chapter 1: Fundamentals of Mass Spectrometry

### 1.1 What is Mass Spectrometry?
Mass spectrometry (MS) is an analytical technique that measures the mass-to-charge ratio (m/z) of ions. It's used to identify and quantify molecules in simple and complex mixtures.

### 1.2 Basic Components
1. Ion Source - converts molecules into ions
2. Mass Analyzer - separates ions by m/z ratio
3. Detector - records the abundance of each ion
